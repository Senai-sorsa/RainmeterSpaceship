CLOVER: ASSUMED SETTINGS AND HOW TO USE EACH FEATURE
=====================================================

Covers the features added in October 2026: follow-up without the wake word, snooze, repeating
reminders, remembered facts, to-do by voice, the daily briefing and the personality setting.

Part 1 lists every choice that was made for you while building them. Nobody asked for these exact
values; they were picked as sensible defaults. "Change in" says where to change one:
  CC    = a row in the Control Center
  code  = a constant in @Resources\Jarvis\jarvis.py (restart CLOVER afterwards)
Part 2 is the how-to for each feature.


PART 1: WHAT WAS ASSUMED
------------------------

FOLLOW-UP WITHOUT THE WAKE WORD
  On by default.                                           CC: FOLLOW-UP  ON / OFF  (JarvisFollowUp)
  At most 3 follow-ups in a row, then "Hey Jarvis" again.  code: "follow < 3" in turn()
    Why: stops him and a television answering each other for ever.
  He waits up to about 6 seconds for you to start talking. code: _listen()
  0.3 second pause before the microphone opens, so he
    does not hear the end of his own voice.                code: time.sleep(0.3) in turn()
  Only after an answer the model worded and he SPOKE.
    No follow-up after fixed replies ("Timer set", "Volume at 30", "Noted: ..."),
    when muted, at volume 0, in STEALTH, or after a cancelled or failed turn.
  No chime and no model warm-up before a follow-up. The hologram shows "listening".
  Privacy: the microphone is open for those seconds after every spoken answer.

SNOOZE
  "Snooze" with no length = 1 minute.                      code: SNOOZE = 60
    You asked for 1 minute. This was read as "the default snooze length".
    NOT built: an alarm that nobody answers does not snooze itself. It rings its 10 times and stops.
  A spoken length must be between 1 minute and 2 hours. Outside that he keeps ringing.
  Words that snooze: snooze, later, not now, "<N> more minutes".
  Snooze is checked before the stop words, so "okay, snooze" snoozes and "okay" stops.
  Snoozing a repeating reminder only delays that one ring. The series carries on as normal.
  Muted or STEALTH: there is no listening during an alarm, so no snooze by voice.

ALARMS IN GENERAL (unchanged, listed so you know them)
  An alarm rings up to 10 times.                           code: RINGS = 10
  Words that stop it: stop, okay, ok, thanks, thank you, quiet, enough, dismiss, shush, shut up.
  More than 2 minutes late (PC was off or asleep): he says "Missed reminder from ...".
  One alarm at a time. Several items due together ring as one alarm.
  Reminders: 5 seconds to 7 days ahead, 20 pending at most.

REPEATING REMINDERS
  Three rules only: every day, every weekday (Mon to Fri), every <one weekday>.
  A clock time is required. "every day in 20 minutes" is refused.
  "at 9" with no am/pm means whichever 9 o'clock comes first.
  The next ring is saved BEFORE he rings, so a crash can never make one ring for ever.
  PC off for several days: it rings once as missed, then carries on from the next real time.
  "9" stays 9 on the wall clock when the clocks change.
  Cancelling removes the whole series.
  Timers never repeat.
  NOT built: every 2 hours, every month, the first Monday, an end date.

REMEMBERED FACTS
  50 facts at most, 200 characters each.                   code: memory_reply()
  At most 2000 characters of them go into each prompt.     code: system_prompt()
  Kept as plain text in @Resources\Data\jarvis_memory.txt. Git ignores that file.
  They are sent only to the local Ollama model.
  He repeats a fact in your own words ("Noted: ..."), the model does not reword it.
  The same fact is not stored twice.
  "Forget <words>" removes every fact that holds those words.
  "Forget everything" asks first and needs a clear yes.
  "Remember to ..." is NOT a fact. It stays a reminder without a time, as before.
  If the file cannot be read he says so and never overwrites it.
  Do not store passwords: anything in the file can be spoken aloud on request.

TO-DO BY VOICE
  He always asks first: "Add '...' to your to-do list? Confirm with yes."
  Only a clear yes within 15 seconds writes. Anything else: "Cancelled."
  The note is the one set in the Control Center (TO-DO NOTE). He never creates a note.
  The line "- [ ] <item>" is added at the END of the note. Existing lines are never rewritten.
  200 characters at most. Line breaks are removed. Capitals are kept as you said them.
  The item text is not written to the diagnostic log.
  NOT built: ticking off or deleting a task, due dates, choosing a heading in the note.

DAILY BRIEFING
  Only when you ask. He never starts talking by himself.
  The model words it, from the data the HUD already shows. Nothing new is read.
  Order: time, today's calendar, unread mail count, open to-dos (three, then the count),
  pending reminders. Five sentences at most. A part with no data is skipped.
  Only whole phrases count: "Good morning, can you open Chrome" is still an app command.
  No weather (it would need your location).

PERSONALITY
  PLAYFUL by default.                                      CC: PERSONALITY  PROPER / PLAYFUL / BRIEF
  It only changes answers the model words. Fixed lines ("Done.", "Time to cook.") never change.
  Not tied to the voice: you pick the voice and the personality separately.

OLDER DEFAULTS THAT THESE FEATURES RELY ON
  He answers after 1 second of silence.                    CC: LISTENING  (JarvisSilence)
  He forgets the conversation after 10 minutes without a turn.
  Model memory is 2048 tokens.                             Settings.inc: JarvisContext
  Risky actions (shut down, close an app, forget everything, add a to-do) need a yes within 15 s.


PART 2: HOW TO USE EACH FEATURE
-------------------------------
Start every first sentence with "Hey Jarvis" or a click on the hologram.

FOLLOW-UP
  Ask something. When he finishes speaking the hologram goes to "listening" again.
  Just talk, no wake word. Say nothing and he goes idle after about 6 seconds.
    You:  Hey Jarvis, how hot is my GPU?
    He:   (answers, then listens)
    You:  And the CPU?
  Turn it off: Control Center, FOLLOW-UP, OFF.

TIMERS AND REMINDERS (as before)
  "Set a timer for 10 minutes."
  "Remind me to cook in 20 minutes."      "Remind me at 5 pm to call the bank."
  "What reminders do I have?"             "What timers do I have?"
  "Cancel the cooking reminder."          "Cancel all reminders."

SNOOZE (only while an alarm is ringing, in the pause after he speaks)
  "Snooze."                  rings again in 1 minute
  "Snooze 5 minutes."        "Ten more minutes."        "Snooze half an hour."
  "Later." / "Not now."      also 1 minute
  "Stop." / "Okay." / "Thanks."   ends the alarm

REPEATING REMINDERS
  "Remind me every day at 9 am to take vitamins."
  "Remind me every weekday at 8:30 to check mail."
  "Remind me to put the bins out every Monday at 7 pm."
  List them:   "What reminders do I have?"   ->  "to take vitamins, every day at 9:00 AM"
  Stop one:    "Cancel the vitamins reminder."   (removes the whole series)

REMEMBERED FACTS
  Save:    "Remember that my dentist is on Fridays."      ->  "Noted: my dentist is on Fridays."
  List:    "What do you remember?"
  Use:     just ask: "When is my dentist?"  He knows it on every later turn.
  Remove:  "Forget the dentist."                          ->  "Forgotten: ..."
  Wipe:    "Forget everything."  then  "Yes."

TO-DO BY VOICE
  First set the note once: Control Center, TO-DO NOTE.
  "Add buy milk to my to-do list."        "Put call the bank on my to-do list."
  "Add a task: renew passport."           "New to-do buy milk."
  He asks "Add 'buy milk' to your to-do list? Confirm with yes."
  "Yes."  ->  "Added."     Anything else  ->  "Cancelled."
  The HUD picks the new line up within a few seconds.
  Hear the list: "What's on my to-do list?"

DAILY BRIEFING
  "Good morning."  "Good afternoon."  "Good evening."
  "Brief me."      "Daily briefing."  "What's my day look like?"

PERSONALITY
  Control Center, PERSONALITY: PROPER (calm and dry), PLAYFUL (warm, teases a little),
  BRIEF (one short sentence).


UNDO
----
  Follow-up:          FOLLOW-UP, OFF.
  Personality:        PERSONALITY, PROPER.
  Remembered facts:   "forget everything", or delete @Resources\Data\jarvis_memory.txt.
  Reminders:          "cancel all reminders", or delete @Resources\Data\jarvis_reminders.txt.
  A to-do line:       delete it in Obsidian.
  The code itself:    git revert the commit that added this file.
